Recommended README sequence: **Overview → Category overview → Transactions → Wallets →
Spending calendar → Savings goals → Reports**. Use the remaining captures in a feature gallery.

For interactive demonstrations, also try:

- Reimport an original CSV: no new rows; then import the overlap scenario: two new rows.
- Preview `import_validation.csv` to show row errors without saving it.
- Compare March with April for the large Toyota payment; compare June with July for holidays.
- Select EUR for a clean view of the Revolut holiday transactions.
- Turn on refund-aware cost analysis to see the May purchase / June refund distinction.
- Select **Phone transaction** or **Internal transfers** in transaction filters.
- Copy or move selected transactions, then undo or discard changes in your disposable copy.
- Generate a July monthly report and the 2026 annual report in the Cockpit design.

All money and quotes are fictional. The screenshots demonstrate ledger and analysis
features; gold and bond wallets are monetary account records, not live asset trackers.
