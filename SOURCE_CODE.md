Keep both source archives available beside the MSI and portable archive, with
equivalent access and at no additional charge. When forwarding this release, forward
the full distribution package, including these archives and legal documents.

Application build instructions: `docs/BUILD_WINDOWS.md` in the source archive.
Third-party packages are unmodified upstream releases; their individual archives
contain their respective build instructions and licenses. Runtime DLLs remain
separate files and may be replaced with compatible modified builds. The application
does not require signature verification of modified copies.

The release manifest identifies the exact files in the application snapshot.

private preferences, generated personal reports or development environment.

Do not distribute a binary as a complete GPL release if its companion sources or
required third-party notices are missing. Do not substitute a newer source snapshot
for the sources corresponding to an older installer.
